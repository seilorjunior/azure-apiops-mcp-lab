# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and welcoming. Review ideas and code, not personal
characteristics. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, disclosure of
private information and deliberately unsafe handling of credentials are not
acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and restrict
participation to protect contributors and users. Report conduct concerns
privately to the repository owner through an existing trusted channel; do not
post another person's private information in an issue.

Use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for technical vulnerability reports.
